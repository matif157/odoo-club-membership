Club Membership Management

Memberships, recurring billing and renewals for clubs, gyms and academies

A clean, tested Odoo 19 module built for real membership businesses in Pakistan and beyond: snooker clubs, gyms, coaching academies and clinics.

  • Membership plans: monthly, quarterly, yearly
  • Automatic end-date calculation with proration-ready structure
  • One-click invoice creation
  • Renewals that keep the full history
  • Overlap protection
  • Daily cron that expires finished memberships
